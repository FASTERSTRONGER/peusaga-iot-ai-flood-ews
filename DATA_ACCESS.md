# Data Availability Statement

## 1. Publicly Available Data in This Repository
To support reproducibility and peer review, this repository provides:
- **`data/sample/`**: Synthetic and anonymized sample datasets generated via `scripts/generate_sample_data.py` with schemas, units, and statistical structures matching the production pipeline.
- **`data/aggregated/`**: Aggregated model evaluation metrics, AHP global criterion weights, mitigation recommendation matrices, and LoRa link-budget/power autonomy simulation summaries.

## 2. Restricted Raw Data
Raw hydrological, meteorological, disaster, and agricultural datasets obtained from third-party institutions (including river basin water-level logs, regional meteorological station records, local disaster agency reports, and farmer group/respondent records) are **not** included in this public repository due to:
1. Institutional data-sharing agreements and usage restrictions.
2. Protection of personally identifiable information (PII) and sensitive field-level identifiers.

## 3. Procedure for Data Access Requests
Researchers seeking access to the underlying raw or de-identified institutional datasets for academic verification may submit a formal request to the corresponding author (Magister Teknologi Informasi, Universitas Malikussaleh) stating the intended research purpose and institutional affiliation. Access is subject to approval from the respective data-providing agencies.
